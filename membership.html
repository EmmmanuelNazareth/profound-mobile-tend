Questions, answered

Is there a contract?

No. Membership is month-to-month. Pause or cancel anytime — we'd rather earn the next month than trap you in it.

What if it rains on my day?

We reschedule to the next clear slot at no charge. Your monthly visits are guaranteed regardless of weather.

Do you really come to me?

Always. We're fully mobile across Baltimore and the DMV — home, office, or job site. We bring everything we need.

Can I roll over a missed visit?

If you travel or can't make a visit, let us know and we'll work it into the following month wherever we can.

Can I upgrade later?

Anytime. Move between plans whenever your needs change — it takes one text.

More than one vehicle?

We offer multi-vehicle and household rates. Mention it when you sign up and we'll build the right plan for your fleet.
